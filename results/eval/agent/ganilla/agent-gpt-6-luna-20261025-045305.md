Tested CUDA float16 autocast, channels_last memory format, fused Adam, and cuDNN autotuning. Each was reverted because benchmarking did not show a reliable speedup with unchanged loss. No optimization was retained.

| # | Change | Before | After | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use CUDA float16 autocast for CycleGAN forward and backward passes to accelerate convolutions and reduce activation bandwidth. | 134.3 ms/step | 140.6 ms/step | reverted: 0.96x is within noise |

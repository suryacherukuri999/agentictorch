# AgenticTorch report: jointbert

**Result: 2.39x faster, loss within noise** on an NVIDIA L4.

- Command: `TOKENIZERS_PARALLELISM=false HF_HOME=hf HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 python main.py --task atis --model_type bert --model_dir atis_model --do_train`
- Baseline: 140.0 ms/step over 2 runs
- Model: gpt-6-luna, 19 turns, 137260 input and 889 output tokens

Fused AdamW and CUDA float16 autocast were retained, improving throughput from 140.0 to 60.1 ms/step (about 2.33x) with loss within noise. Deferred loss synchronization and pinned/nonblocking DataLoader transfers did not beat the best version beyond noise and were reverted. No further promising changes remain within the benchmark budget.

## Changes

| # | Change | Before | After | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enable fused AdamW to combine optimizer math into fewer GPU kernels. | 136.6 ms/step | 118.5 ms/step | kept: 1.15x faster, loss within noise |
| 2 | Use CUDA float16 autocast for transformer forward and loss to accelerate tensor-core operations. | 119.5 ms/step | 60.5 ms/step | kept: 1.98x faster, loss within noise |
| 3 | Accumulate detached loss on device and synchronize once at training end instead of calling loss.item() every step. | 60.1 ms/step | 59.9 ms/step | reverted: 1.00x is within noise |
| 4 | Pin training batches and use nonblocking host-to-device copies to allow asynchronous transfers. | 62.3 ms/step | 60.1 ms/step | reverted: 1.04x is within noise |

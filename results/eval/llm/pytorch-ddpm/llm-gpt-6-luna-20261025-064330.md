# AgenticTorch report: pytorch-ddpm

**Result: 1.37x faster, loss within noise** on an NVIDIA L4.

- Command: `python main.py --train --flagfile ./config/CIFAR10.txt`
- Baseline: 550.5 ms/step over 2 runs
- Model: gpt-6-luna, 8 turns, 66093 input and 680 output tokens

Enabled CUDA bfloat16 autocast for the training forward pass, cuDNN benchmarking for fixed-shape convolutions, pinned-memory/nonblocking data transfers, persistent DataLoader workers, and fused Adam. These preserve the training setup and outputs while reducing compute, optimizer, and input-pipeline overhead.

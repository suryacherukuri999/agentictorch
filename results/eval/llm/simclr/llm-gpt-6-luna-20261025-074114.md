# AgenticTorch report: simclr

**Result: 1.62x faster, loss within noise** on an NVIDIA L4.

- Command: `python main.py`
- Baseline: 422.0 ms/step over 2 runs
- Model: gpt-6-luna, 6 turns, 36818 input and 1260 output tokens

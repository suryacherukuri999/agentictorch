# AgenticTorch report: nerf-pytorch

**Result: 2.49x faster, loss within noise** on an NVIDIA L4.

- Command: `python run_nerf.py --config configs/lego.txt`
- Baseline: 174.2 ms/step over 2 runs
- Model: gpt-6-luna, 13 turns, 233031 input and 1932 output tokens

Enabled CUDA bfloat16 autocast for the NeRF render/model forward pass, compiled the coarse and fine networks on CUDA, and used optimizer.zero_grad(set_to_none=True) to reduce gradient-reset overhead. Kept loss computation in float32 outside autocast for numerical stability and preserved the training objective and logging.
